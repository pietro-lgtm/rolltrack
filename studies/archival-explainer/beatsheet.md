# Beat sheet — "In God We Trust" (frame-by-frame recreation study)

Recreation of the @hex_creatives reel structure from the archival-explainer-animation skill.
9:16 (1080×1920), 60 s, 30 fps. English. Silent draft (captions carry the script).

Facts verified: "Under God" added to the Pledge June 14, 1954 (P.L. 83-396) after Rev. George
Docherty's Feb 7, 1954 sermon at New York Avenue Presbyterian Church, Washington, with President
Eisenhower in the pew. "In God We Trust" became national motto July 30, 1956. First paper money
with the motto: series 1957 $1 silver certificate. Coins carried it earlier (1864) — omitted for time.

## Style checks (from the skill)
- No shot holds still > 4 s — every beat has a push or an event.
- Color only on: green bill, yellow boxes/highlights, red threat box.
- Captions ≤ 4 words except emphasis stacks; word-by-word reveal.
- Serif: EB Garamond (upright book serif per Pietro's rules — no Instrument Serif, no all-caps subtitles except on-document text).
